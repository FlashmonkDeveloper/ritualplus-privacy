Privacy Policy

Ritual+: Habit Tracker, Focus · Effective 22 October 2026

Ritual+ keeps nothing. There are no accounts, no servers and no analytics. Your rituals, journal and history live only on your phone, and we could not see them even if we wanted to.

What we collect

Nothing. Ritual+ does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data. The app has no sign-in, does not connect to our own servers, and contains no analytics or crash-reporting software.

What stays on your phone

Everything you enter is stored only in the app's private storage on your device:

  • Your rituals, their schedules, reminders, steps and notes
  • Your check-ins, history, journal entries and focus minutes
  • Your settings, such as language, appearance and passcode (the passcode is stored only as a salted, one-way hash)

Uninstalling the app, or choosing Erase all data in Settings, deletes this data from the device.

Backups

  • Backups you export. Settings → Export backup creates a backup file that you save wherever you choose. It is a plain file, so keep it somewhere you trust. We never receive a copy.
  • Android's own backup. If you have turned on Android backup on your phone, Android may include Ritual+'s data in your device backup to your Google account, and restore it on a new phone. This is handled by Android and Google under your Google account settings, not by us; we cannot access it. Your passcode and device-only settings are excluded from these backups.

Permissions and why they are used

  • Notifications: to show the reminders you set.
  • Alarms & reminders (exact alarms): so reminders and focus timers arrive at the exact minute.
  • Full-screen alerts: for alarm-style reminders that ring and fill the screen, like an alarm clock.
  • Run at startup: to set your reminders again after the phone restarts.
  • Vibration: for reminder alerts and gentle feedback.
  • Biometrics (fingerprint): only if you choose fingerprint unlock. The check is done entirely by Android; Ritual+ never receives or stores your fingerprint.

Ritual+ does not use the internet, your location, contacts, camera, microphone or files beyond the backup file you choose to export or import.

Children

Ritual+ is not directed at children under 13 and collects no information from anyone, including children.

Your responsibility

Because your data lives only on your device, keeping it private is in your hands. If other people use your phone, set a passcode in Settings → Privacy, and store exported backups somewhere safe.

Changes to this policy

If a future version changes how data is handled, for example if optional paid features are added through Google Play, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the date above will change.
